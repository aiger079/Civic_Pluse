Review the links and supporting context behind a fact-check before sharing or acting on a claim.

02 / BROWSING

Check as you read

Optional visible-text scanning can flag selected claims on supported sites. You can turn it off in the extension.

03 / CONTROL

Your call, every time

A red highlight is a review prompt from an AI system, not proof. Read the evidence and decide for yourself.
